01 / FICA & KYC Compliance

Customer Due Diligence & Compliance Framework

Developing a practical customer onboarding and due diligence framework for a fictional financial services institution.

FICA KYC CDD & EDD PEP Screening Risk-Based Approach
Explore full project

01. Business Scenario

A fictional financial services institution is experiencing inconsistent customer onboarding practices. Some customer files contain incomplete identification records, risk assessments are not consistently documented and screening results are not always retained.

02. Regulatory & Governance Context

The proposed framework considers the Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA), applicable Financial Intelligence Centre guidance and the institution's internal risk-based approach.

03. Sample Policy Framework

A proposed Customer Due Diligence Policy would cover:

  • Customer identification and verification requirements.
  • Understanding the purpose and intended nature of the business relationship.
  • Beneficial ownership identification where applicable.
  • Customer risk classification and ongoing due diligence.
  • PEP and sanctions screening procedures.
  • Enhanced due diligence and escalation requirements.
  • Record-keeping and periodic customer reviews.

04. Policy Implementation Plan

  1. Review applicable legislation, guidance and regulatory obligations.
  2. Identify gaps in existing onboarding procedures.
  3. Draft or update the customer due diligence policy and supporting procedures.
  4. Obtain appropriate management approval before implementation.
  5. Communicate requirements and train relevant employees.
  6. Introduce standardised checklists and documented approval processes.
  7. Monitor implementation through file reviews and compliance testing.

02 / AML & FINANCIAL CRIME Financial Crime

Transaction Monitoring & Suspicious Activity Review

Examining fictional transaction activity, identifying potential money laundering indicators and developing an investigation and escalation workflow.

AML Transaction Monitoring Red Flags FIC Reporting
Explore full project

01. Business Scenario

A fictional customer account receives multiple deposits from unrelated third parties over a short period. Funds are subsequently transferred to another account. The activity appears inconsistent with the customer's known profile.

02. Potential Risk Indicators

  • Unusual transaction frequency or value.
  • Multiple unrelated third-party deposits.
  • Rapid movement of funds between accounts.
  • Activity inconsistent with the customer's known occupation or expected profile.
  • Unclear economic purpose for the transactions.

03. Investigation Workflow

  1. Review the monitoring alert and available customer information.
  2. Examine transaction history and identify unusual patterns.
  3. Compare activity with the customer's expected transaction profile.
  4. Consider available explanations and supporting documentation.
  5. Record findings, evidence and unresolved questions.
  6. Escalate concerns to the appropriate compliance officer or authorised function.
  7. Consider applicable reporting obligations under FICA.

04. Reporting Considerations

The relevant reporting obligation depends on the facts and circumstances. The project considers suspicious transaction reporting under section 29 of FICA, as well as applicable cash threshold and other reporting requirements where relevant.

An unusual transaction is not automatically proof of criminal conduct. Reporting decisions must be assessed against the applicable legal requirements and internal escalation procedures.

Suspicious Activity Investigation Log
AML Red Flag Assessment Checklist
Investigation Findings Report
Escalation & Reporting Decision Log

06. Systems & Tools to Explore

  • Transaction monitoring systems: identifying activity requiring review.
  • Case management systems: recording alerts, investigations and outcomes.
  • Microsoft Excel: analysing fictional transaction data and identifying patterns.
  • Screening platforms: supporting relevant customer and counterparty screening.

07. Monitoring & Quality Assurance

Review alert handling, investigation documentation, escalation timelines, quality of case records and completion of follow-up actions.

Key Learning Outcome

03 / RISK & GOVERNANCE Governance

Compliance Risk Management & Policy Implementation

Developing a practical compliance risk register, governance framework and policy monitoring plan for a fictional organisation.

King V King IV Risk Registers Internal Controls Policy Monitoring
Explore full project

01. Business Scenario

A fictional organisation identifies weaknesses in its compliance processes. Internal policies are outdated, employees are unclear about their responsibilities, compliance reviews are inconsistent and identified issues are not always resolved.

02. Governance Framework

This project considers the South African corporate governance framework, including King IV as historical background and King V as the current governance reference.

The project explores ethical leadership, risk oversight, compliance, accountability, internal controls and the role of governing bodies in providing oversight.

03. Sample Compliance Risk Register

Identified Risk Proposed Control
Outdated policies Scheduled policy reviews and approvals
Incomplete customer records File reviews and documentation checklists
Insufficient employee awareness Training and policy acknowledgements
Unresolved compliance findings Corrective action tracking and escalation

04. Policy Implementation Lifecycle

  1. Identify applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  2. Conduct a compliance gap assessment.
  3. Draft or revise the relevant policies and procedures.
  4. Obtain approval from the appropriate authority.
  5. Communicate policies and responsibilities to employees.
  6. Provide training and retain acknowledgement records.
  7. Monitor compliance through testing and reviews.
  8. Record findings, assign corrective actions and track closure.
  9. Review policies periodically and when regulatory changes occur.

Monitor policy review dates, overdue corrective actions, compliance findings, employee training completion and outstanding control weaknesses. Prepare management reporting to support oversight and accountability.
